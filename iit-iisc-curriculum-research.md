# IIT/IISc BTech+MTech CS & Math Curriculum Research — Consolidated Citation Backing

This file is the citation trail for `unified-curriculum.md` §17.1.17 (`SRC-CURR-001`
through `SRC-CURR-006`) and `research-audit-log.md` Batch B17. It consolidates six
per-institute research passes (IIT Bombay, Delhi, Kanpur, Kharagpur, Madras, IISc),
each covering that institute's BTech/BE (or BS-equivalent) and MTech/ME (or
MSc-equivalent) Computer Science and Mathematics curricula. Access date for every
source below: 2026-09-08.

Excluded throughout: Scribd, LibGen, Z-Library, Sci-Hub, Internet Archive full-text/
borrow, epdf.pub, PDF Drive, dokumen.pub, and general aggregator/coaching sites
(Careers360, Shiksha, Collegedunia, Zollege, Quora, CourseHero, university-kart-style
sites). None of these were fetched or cited as content sources anywhere below —
only where they appeared in search results and were explicitly rejected is that
rejection logged, per institute, so the discipline is auditable.

---

## SRC-CURR-001 — IIT Bombay

**Primary sources (official, `iitb.ac.in` subdomains):**
- `cse.iitb.ac.in/~rkj/ugcurriculum07batch.pdf` — CSE UG Curriculum, 2007-batch (full B.Tech CSE structure, credits, electives, Honours/Dual-Degree/Minor rules)
- `cse.iitb.ac.in/~sb/facad/curriculum.html` — M.Tech CSE credit-structure page
- `cse.iitb.ac.in/~siva/talks/mtech-curr.pdf` — M.Tech CSE program slide deck (2004; structural/compulsory-course description only)
- `cse.iitb.ac.in/~cs601/lec.html` — CS601 Algorithms and Complexity, live course page
- `math.iitb.ac.in/Academics/courses/Course-Curriculum-Booklet.pdf` — Dept of Mathematics Course Curriculum Booklet, dated 2025-09-03 (current, official): B.S. Mathematics, Integrated M.Sc., dual-degree M.Sc. Math/Statistics conversions, IDDDP, full per-course syllabi (MA401/403/406/408/410/412/414/417/419/450/503/504/510/515)

**Rejected/unusable:** Scribd documents (multiple, on B.Tech/M.Tech CSE and IDD Math+Computing syllabi); collegedunia.com, universitykart.com, careers360.com, coursehero.com, iitbcse.blogspot.com; IIT (BHU) Varanasi pages (wrong institute); `cse.iitb.ac.in/academics/*` (JS single-page app, returned only page shell); Institute-wide MTech admissions brochure (used only tangentially, superseded by department page).

**Key finding:** IIT Bombay has **no program named "Mathematics and Computing."** Its Math dept runs a B.S. in Mathematics (4-year) with dual-degree M.Sc. Math/Statistics extensions. Graduate math is a 2-year **M.Sc.**, not an M.Tech. B.Tech CSE per-course textbooks are not published officially anywhere located; only the Math dept publishes full per-course topic+textbook syllabi.

(JAM-admitted); 8 of 14 of its listed compulsory course codes could not be cross-verified against the 2024 DOAA catalog (flagged as likely renumbering, not guessed).

**Rejected/unusable:** Scribd documents (multiple); careers360.com, zollege.in; a third-party S3-hosted PDF describing an unverifiable "Mathematics and Computing (M.Sc. 5Y)" variant — not used, hence that variant is flagged as a gap rather than reported.

**Key finding:** "Mathematics and Computing" is a confirmed 4-year **B.S.** (splCode MA2) run by the Math dept — full course list found but no topic/textbook detail anywhere official. M.Tech CSE core codes in the live ERP (CS60005/CS60007) don't match the 2009 syllabus PDF's codes (CS60001/CS60003) — likely a renumbering; content very likely still accurate, flagged as a version-currency caveat. No M.Tech Mathematics exists (only M.Sc. Mathematics, splCode MA4). The already-integrated IIT Kharagpur EPGC (GenAI certificate) program was explicitly excluded and not re-fetched.

## SRC-CURR-006 — IISc

**Primary sources (official, `iisc.ac.in` subdomains):**
- `csa.iisc.ac.in/academics-all/degree-programs/`, `.../courses/`, `.../courses/current/`, `academics/courses/descriptions` — CSA degree structure, course numbering, current offerings, ~80 course descriptions with topics+textbooks
- `csa.iisc.ac.in/newweb/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Information-Brochure-2020.pdf` — CSA Information Brochure 2020 (M.Tech CSE/Research/PhD structure, Pool A/B/C course lists with topics+textbooks for ~35 courses, e.g. E0222 Automata Theory: Hopcroft & Ullman, Kozen, Thomas; E0224 Computational Complexity: **Arora & Barak**; E0235 Cryptography: Stinson, Menezes et al.)
- `math.iisc.ac.in`, `course-list.html`, `catalogue.html` — Mathematics dept programs and course catalogue with topics/references
- `bs-ug.iisc.ac.in`, `.../course-structure/mathematics` — BS(Research) program structure, six Majors (no CS), Mathematics major course structure
- `iisc.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/UG-Handbook-2024-25-signed.pdf` — official UG Handbook: BS(Research) structure, B.Tech (Mathematics and Computing) [Ch.3/17], M.Tech (Mathematics and Computing) [Ch.4], full BS(Research) Math major course list with topics+textbooks (UMA101/102/201, UM204/205, MA200/212/213/219/222/224/231/241, citing Apostol, Strang, Tao, Artin, Dummit & Foote, Munkres, Armstrong, Royden, Folland, Ahlfors, Conway, Stein & Shakarchi, Hartman, Coddington & Levinson)

**Rejected/unusable:** Quora, Careers360, Shiksha, and other aggregator sites; `csa.iisc.ac.in/academics/academics-courses.php` (HTTP 500, server error, not a content rejection); `eecs.iisc.ac.in/mtechai/`/`ai.iisc.ac.in/mtechai/` (official iisc.ac.in subdomains, but used only to establish departmental ownership of M.Tech(AI), not fetched for a detailed curriculum table, since ownership was ambiguous across sources).

**Key finding (corrects the task's premise):** IISc's BS(Research) programme has exactly six Majors (Biology, Chemistry, Earth & Env. Sci, Materials, Mathematics, Physics) — **no Computer Science major or minor exists.** But IISc separately runs a **B.Tech (Mathematics and Computing)** degree (UG Handbook Ch.3/17), administered outside BS(Research) — the closest thing to an undergraduate CS curriculum at IISc, contradicting the premise that "IISc does not offer a B.Tech." M.Tech CSA (CSE) is fully documented via the 2020 Information Brochure. **No standalone M.Tech/M.Sc(Engg./Research) Mathematics exists** — only Integrated PhD/PhD, plus the 5th-year M.Tech(Mathematics and Computing) tied to the B.Tech(M&C) program.
