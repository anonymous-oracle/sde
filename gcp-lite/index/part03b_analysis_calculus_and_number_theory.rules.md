# B. Directives for this domain

## Calculus, Analysis, Coordinate Geometry & Number Theory

**Prerequisites:** school-level algebra, trigonometry and coordinate geometry (part 01); proof techniques, induction and modular arithmetic, linear algebra, and limits, derivatives and integrals at the level of part 03.

**Depth bar:** JEE Advanced problem fluency in calculus and coordinate geometry, then a first proof-based course in real and complex analysis and elementary number theory: every theorem proved, every technique derived and applied to unseen problems; the landmark results at statement level with a special case proved and a computational experiment.

**Capstone:** a long unseen problem set at qualifying-examination difficulty across all groups, with a written report and an oral defence, plus a Go package that verifies results numerically: series convergence, an ODE solver checked against closed forms, Miller–Rabin, continued fractions and Pell-equation solutions.
