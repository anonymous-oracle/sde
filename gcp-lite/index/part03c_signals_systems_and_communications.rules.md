# B. Directives for this domain

## Signals, Systems & Communications

**Prerequisites:** complex numbers and differential equations (part 03b); linear algebra, probability and random variables, and the signals and sequences group (part 03); Python with NumPy and Matplotlib (part 05).

**Depth bar:** a first graduate course in digital signal processing and an undergraduate course in digital communications: every transform property derived, every filter designed by hand and then in code, and every result measured on real or simulated signals against theory.

**Capstone:** a software receiver built from scratch in Python or Go: filter design, resampling, modulation and demodulation over a simulated AWGN and fading channel, synchronization and equalization, with the bit-error rate measured against the theoretical curve, a written report and an oral defence.
