The Multimodal and Reasoning Era
The frontier bifurcated along two axes. On the input side, models became natively multimodal: GPT-4 (2023) accepted images; Gemini (2023) was
trained on text, images, audio, and video end-to-end; Claude 3 and 4 offered long-context vision-language reasoning.

On the reasoning side, a new generation of systems — OpenAI's o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, and similar — spent significant inference-time compute on
chain-of-thought search, optimized via reinforcement learning on verifiable outcomes. For the first time, models routinely solved competition-level
mathematics and programming problems.
