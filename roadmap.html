  • Then B3 to B5, then B6: the two builds (truth-table checker, DFA simulator), a problem set, and the assignment

When does an assignment come? Each unit's assignment sits at the end of that unit. A1.1 arrives after B6, the last block of U1.1, so after B3, B4, B5 and the builds. There is no calendar date: you advance by passing checks. A1.1 is five questions worth 10 marks, with a pass at 7/10 and both the proof question and the programming question accepted. After that, one assignment follows each unit that has one (148 in total; the 7 capstones have projects instead).
